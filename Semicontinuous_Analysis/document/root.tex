\title{Semicontinuity: Envelopes and Berge's Theorem}
\author{Dmitriy Traytel}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  Arguments in optimal control and in the theory of viscosity solutions work
  with functions that are only upper or lower semicontinuous, and repeatedly
  need their extrema on compacta, their semicontinuous envelopes, and the
  regularity of a value obtained by optimising over a compact set.  This
  entry assembles that toolkit for real-valued functions on a metric space,
  on top of the extended-real theory of the Archive entry
  \texttt{Lower\_Semicontinuous}.  We formalize the
  $\varepsilon$-$\delta$ calculus of semicontinuity, attainment of extrema on
  nonempty compacta, bounded extension off a closed set, the envelopes taken
  over the whole space and relative to a closed set, and Berge's maximum
  theorem, that the value of a maximisation depends upper semicontinuously on
  its parameter.  The envelope is shown to be the hull of
  \texttt{Lower\_Semicontinuous} transported to the reals, so its basic
  theory is inherited rather than repeated.
\end{abstract}

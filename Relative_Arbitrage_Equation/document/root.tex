\title{A Degenerate Elliptic Equation under Eigenvalue Constraints}
\author{Dmitriy Traytel}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  The deterministic part of Lai, Shkolnikov and
  Soner~\cite{LaiShkolnikovSoner}: the operator $F$ of their Eq.~(1.9), a
  supremum of $-\frac12\operatorname{tr}(aM)$ over positive semidefinite
  matrices $a$ with $\operatorname{tr}(a)=1$ and $k$ eigenvalues bounded
  below, its exact form (Lemma~2.1), its semicontinuous envelopes and the
  viscosity notions of their Definition~3.1, the subspace-tangential field
  used for their Example~3.1, and the comparison principle of their
  Section~4. No probability theory is used.
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}
  The formal statement of Theorem 1.1 of Lai, Shkolnikov and Soner,
  \emph{Relative arbitrage problem under eigenvalue lower bounds}
  (arXiv:2512.17702), together with every definition it mentions, so that the
  formal statement can be checked against the paper without reading the
  proofs.  Nothing is proved here that is not proved in the accompanying
  development: each clause is restated in full and discharged by a single
  reference to it.  This document is generated in outline mode, so those
  references are elided and only the statements remain.
\end{abstract}

\title{Theorem 1.1 of arXiv:2512.17702, as Formalised}
\author{Dmitriy Traytel}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  The formal statement of Theorem 1.1 of Lai, Shkolnikov and Soner,
  \emph{Relative arbitrage problem under eigenvalue lower bounds}
  (arXiv:2512.17702), together with every definition specific to the paper
  that its statement mentions, so that the formal statement can be checked
  against the paper without reading the proofs.  Notions belonging to
  Isabelle's own libraries --- martingales, suprema, semicontinuity, and the
  like --- are used but not restated.  Nothing is proved here that is not
  proved in the accompanying development: the statement is discharged by a
  single reference to it.  Proofs are omitted from this document.
\end{abstract}

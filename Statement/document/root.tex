\title{Theorem 1.1 of arXiv:2512.17702, as Formalised}
\author{Dmitriy Traytel}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent\looseness=-1
The formal statement of Theorem 1.1 from ``Relative arbitrage problem under eigenvalue lower bounds'' by Lai, Shkolnikov, and Soner~\cite{LaiShkolnikovSoner}, together with every definition specific to the paper that its statement mentions, so that the formal statement can be checked against the paper without reading the proofs.  Notions belonging to Isabelle's own libraries --- martingales, suprema, semicontinuity, and the like --- are used but not restated.  Proofs are omitted from this document.
\end{abstract}

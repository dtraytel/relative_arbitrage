\title{Martingales with Constrained Covariation and the Minimum Exit Time}
\author{Dmitriy Traytel}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  Fix a set $S$ of $n \times n$ matrices and consider the martingale laws
  on the space of continuous paths in $\mathbb{R}^n$, started at a point,
  whose covariation has its density in $S$; the minimum-exit-time problem
  asks for the supremum over this class of the essential infimum of the
  exit time from a set $K$.  On top of the operations on path laws of the
  Archive entry \texttt{Path\_Space\_Operations}, which defines the class,
  this entry develops its theory with $S$ as a parameter, each result
  taking from $S$ only what it uses.  For a closed $S$ with a norm bound
  and a bound on the diagonal, the class is tight and closed under weak
  limits, hence weakly compact; so it is relatively compact when $S$ is a
  bounded set of positive semidefinite matrices and compact when $S$ is a
  compact one.  Brownian motion pushed through a matrix $\sigma$ with
  $\sigma\sigma^{T} \in S$ witnesses that the class is nonempty.  For a
  convex $S$ the class is closed under shortening the horizon and under
  pasting a member to a kernel of continuations; the value is upper
  semicontinuous, Borel measurable and attained, with a measurable optimal
  selector.  Conditioning on the past at a stopping time and the additive
  glue at a stopping time then give the dynamic programming principle,
  with attainment, at a deterministic time and at a stopping time, in the
  form used by Lai, Shkolnikov and Soner~\cite{LaiShkolnikovSoner} after
  Larsson and Ruf~\cite{LarssonRuf}.
\end{abstract}

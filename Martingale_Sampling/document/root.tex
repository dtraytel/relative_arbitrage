\title{Optional Sampling and Quadratic Variation for Continuous-Time Martingales}
\author{Dmitriy Traytel}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  A toolbox of martingale results that the Archive's existing entries do not
  provide, developed on top of the \texttt{Martingales} entry.

  Doob's maximal inequality is proved for discrete-time martingales, and with
  it a running-maximum bound usable as a dominating function.  Optional
  sampling is proved first for integrable discrete-time martingales, then for
  a continuous-time martingale sampled on a grid, then at a simple stopping
  time, and finally at an arbitrary bounded stopping time, by approximating
  the stopping time from above by its dyadic ceilings; the localised form,
  over an event of the past, is proved along the way.  Optional stopping
  follows: the stopped process is again a martingale.

  Quadratic variation of a square-integrable martingale is defined together
  with its compensator, and the sampled process on a partition is shown to be
  a square-integrable discrete-time martingale whose compensator is the
  sampled one, which is what lets a continuous-time statement be reduced to a
  discrete one.

  Finally, uniform integrability is defined and Vitali's convergence theorem
  proved, together with the practical criterion that a uniform bound on a
  higher moment suffices, and the uniform integrability of a family of
  conditional expectations of a single integrable function.  See Karatzas and
  Shreve~\cite{KaratzasShreve} and Williams~\cite{Williams} for the textbook
  accounts.
\end{abstract}
